# KTV Working Drone Thailand - Market Research

## Company Overview

### KTV Group Background

- **Origin**: Norwegian company, part of KTV Group
- **Founded**: 1992 by Kennet Nilsen
- **Specialization**: Autonomous facade cleaning drones
- **Global Status**: Only certified provider of autonomous facade cleaning drones globally
- **Business Model**: Franchise model across 66 countries
- **Thailand**: Country partner announced

### Core Services

| Service | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Facade Cleaning | Autonomous drone-based exterior building cleaning |
| Window Cleaning | High-rise and commercial window cleaning |
| Solar Panel Cleaning | Drone-assisted solar array maintenance |
| Infrastructure Cleaning | Bridges, towers, and large-scale infrastructure |
| Drone Inspections | Visual and sensor-based structural inspections |
| Surface Treatment | Coating, sealing, and protective surface applications |
| 3D Mapping | High-resolution spatial mapping and modeling |

### Notable Achievements

- First drone to clean an airport (Bergen Airport, Norway)
- Cleaned a 112-meter building in Oslo
- Cleaned the Icon of the Seas (Royal Caribbean cruise ship)

## Regulatory Environment

### CAAT (Civil Aviation Authority of Thailand) Regulations

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Registration | Required for all drones weighing more than 250g |
| Maximum Altitude | 90 meters above ground level |
| Airport Buffer Zone | 9 kilometers from airport boundaries |
| Operating Hours | 06:00 - 18:00 only |

### Foreign Ownership Restrictions

| Pathway | Ownership Limit | Notes |
|---------|-----------------|-------|
| Foreign Business Act (FBA) | 49% maximum foreign ownership | Requires Thai majority shareholder(s) |
| BOI Promotion | 100% foreign ownership permitted | Must qualify for Board of Investment promotion |
