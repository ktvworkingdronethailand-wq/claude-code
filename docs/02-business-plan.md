# KTV Working Drone Thailand - Business Plan

## Executive Summary

### Mission

To deliver professional, technology-driven drone services across Thailand, combining Norwegian operational excellence with deep local market knowledge.

### Vision

To become Thailand's leading integrated drone services provider, setting the industry standard for safety, quality, and innovation.

### Value Proposition

End-to-end drone services backed by ISO-certified processes, cutting-edge autonomous technology, and a franchise network spanning 66 countries, tailored to Thailand's agricultural, industrial, and urban markets.
