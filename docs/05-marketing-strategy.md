## Cultural Considerations for Thai Market

| Factor | Guidance |
|--------|----------|
| Relationship-First | Business in Thailand is built on personal relationships and trust before transactions |
| LINE is Essential | LINE is not optional; it is the primary business and personal communication tool in Thailand |
| Respect Seniority | Observe hierarchical norms in all business interactions, address senior stakeholders appropriately |
| Avoid Confrontation | Thai business culture values harmony; address disagreements diplomatically and privately |
