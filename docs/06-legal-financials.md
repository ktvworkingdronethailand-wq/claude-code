### Capital Requirements

- **Minimum Recommended Capital**: THB 3 - 5 million

## Regulatory Compliance

### CAAT (Civil Aviation Authority of Thailand)

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Drone Registration | All commercial drones must be registered with CAAT |
| Pilot Licensing | CAAT-issued remote pilot license required for commercial operations |
| UAOC | Unmanned Aircraft Operating Certificate required for commercial operators |
| Altitude Limit | Maximum 90 meters above ground level |
| BVLOS Operations | Beyond Visual Line of Sight requires special CAAT approval |

### Agricultural Regulations

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Pesticide Applicator License | Required for all agricultural spraying operations |
| Chemical Storage | Must comply with hazardous material storage regulations |
| Buffer Zone - Waterways | Minimum 30 meters from waterways |
| Buffer Zone - Residential | Minimum 50 meters from residential areas |
| Buffer Zone - Schools | Minimum 100 meters from schools and educational facilities |

### PDPA (Personal Data Protection Act) Compliance

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| DPO Appointment | Data Protection Officer must be designated |
| Aerial Photography Consent | Consent required for capturing identifiable imagery |
| Data Breach Reporting | Mandatory notification within 72 hours of a data breach |
