## Market Opportunity

Drone facade cleaning is rapidly emerging as a new standard for high-rise building maintenance driven by:

- **Cost** — Lower labour risk and reduced insurance liability
- **ESG** — Sustainable maintenance practices aligned with green building standards
- **Regulation** — Increasing workplace safety requirements in Thailand
