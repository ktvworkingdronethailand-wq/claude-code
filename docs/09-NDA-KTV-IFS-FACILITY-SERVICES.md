**IFS Facility Services Co., Ltd.** (Thailand - Head Office)

Collectively referred to as **"the Parties."**

## WHEREAS

The Parties are exploring the possibility of engaging in one or more mutually beneficial future business relationships (collectively, the **"Purpose"**). The Parties recognize that in the course of their discussion to further the Purpose, it will be necessary for each Party to disclose to the other certain Proprietary and Confidential Information (as defined below). Each Party desires to set forth the terms that apply to such Confidential Information.

**NOW, THEREFORE,** the Parties do hereby agree as follows:

---

## 1. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all non-public, proprietary, technical, commercial, financial, or business information disclosed by either Party, whether oral, written, graphic, electronic, or in any other form, including but not limited to:

- Business plans
- Technology and drone operations
- Software and data
- ESG reports
- Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
- Client lists
- Building information and inspection reports
- Images and recordings
- Pricing and contracts
- Trade secrets

---

## 2. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

- Is or becomes public knowledge through no fault of the Receiving Party;
- Is lawfully obtained from a third party without restriction;
- Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to the Disclosing Party's information;
- Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order (with prompt notice to the Disclosing Party, if permitted).

---

## 3. Obligations of Receiving Party

The Receiving Party shall:

- Use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating or pursuing a business relationship;
- Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party except to its employees, contractors, or advisors who have a need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as strict as this Agreement;
- Take all reasonable steps to protect Confidential Information and prevent unauthorized use or disclosure;
- Not copy, reproduce, or use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the Purpose without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party.

---

## 4. Ownership

The disclosure of Confidential Information from Disclosing Party to Receiving Party shall not change the ownership therein. Any Confidential Information owned by Disclosing Party prior to disclosure to Receiving Party shall remain the property of Disclosing Party.

No license or other rights to Confidential Information is granted or implied to Receiving Party hereby, other than the rights expressly stated in this Agreement.

---

## 5. Term & Survival

This Agreement is effective on the date above and continues for a period of **three (3) years** from the date of last disclosure, or until Confidential Information ceases to be confidential under Section 2, whichever is sooner.

---

## 6. Data Ownership

Any inspection data, reports, images, recordings, building condition information, or other documentation generated in connection with discussions, demonstrations, or pilot activities relating to the Purpose shall remain the property of **IFS Facility Services Co., Ltd.** or the relevant building owner, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

The Receiving Party shall not use, disclose, publish, or commercially exploit such information without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party.

---

## 7. Non-Circumvention

The Receiving Party acknowledges that during discussions relating to the Purpose it may become aware of clients, building owners, occupiers, or business opportunities introduced by the Disclosing Party.

The Receiving Party agrees that it shall not, without prior written consent of the Disclosing Party:

- Directly approach;
- Solicit business from; or
- Enter into commercial discussions with

any such client, building owner, or occupier introduced by the Disclosing Party.

This restriction shall remain in effect for **twenty-four (24) months** following the last disclosure of Confidential Information.

---

## 8. Remedies

The Parties acknowledge that unauthorized disclosure may cause irreparable harm, entitling the Disclosing Party to seek injunctive relief, in addition to any other available remedies.

---

## 9. No License or IP Transfer

Nothing in this Agreement grants any right, title, or license to the Receiving Party under any intellectual property or other rights of the Disclosing Party.

---

## 10. Return or Destruction

Upon written request, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information, including all copies, and certify such destruction or return in writing.

---

## 11. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the **Kingdom of Thailand**.

Any disputes shall be resolved by:

1. Good faith negotiation;
2. If unresolved, escalation to executive management; and
3. Finally, by arbitration in **Bangkok, Thailand**.

---

## 12. Miscellaneous

- This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter.
- Any amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
- If any provision is held invalid, the remainder shall remain in effect.

---

The Parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.
