### Critical Issue: 90m Altitude Limit

- CAAT max altitude: 90m AGL
- Tallest tower: ~430m
- **Without waiver: can only service lower 20-25% of tallest tower**

**Strategy: Apply for "structure-proximate operations" waiver**
- Drone stays within 5m of building facade at all times
- Not free flight — building-attached operational mode
- Tethered operations where feasible
- GPS geofencing to prevent lateral departure
- Precedent likely exists for telecom tower inspections
