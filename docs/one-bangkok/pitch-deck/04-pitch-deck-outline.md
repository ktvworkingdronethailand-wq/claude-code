## Objection Handling

### "Drones can't clean as well as traditional methods"
Drone cleaning is measurably MORE consistent — pre-programmed paths, identical pressure, full coverage. We welcome a side-by-side test.

### "What about wind at height?"
Rated for 15 m/s (54 km/h) — same threshold as gondola operations. Real-time anemometer data at flight altitude for go/no-go decisions.

### "What if a drone fails?"
Redundant systems (motors, GPS, comms). Auto return-to-home. 16-drone fleet means instant replacement. Zero-incident record.

### "Why exclusive?"
Data integrity requires consistent collection. IFS integration is a one-time build. One SLA, one accountability. KTV invests in One Bangkok-specific configurations.

### "What's your track record?"
Norwegian offshore heritage. Commercial operations in Thailand. 90-day pilot eliminates risk — let us prove it on one tower.
