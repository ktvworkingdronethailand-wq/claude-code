## 5. Deal Acceleration Tactics

1. **Free pilot** — zero-cost 2-week demo removes procurement barrier
2. **Norwegian reference** — formal letter from parent company
3. **IFS CEO endorsement** — institutional credibility at presentation
4. **PCS warm introduction** — leverage existing FM relationship
5. **First-mover framing** — "exclusive for the first to commit"
6. **Board-level network** — IFS global or Norwegian connections to TCC

## 6. Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We have FM contractors" | "We're not replacing — we're upgrading. PCS stays for ground FM." |
| "Drones aren't proven in Thailand" | "Standard in Europe/Japan/Australia. Pilot proves it on YOUR building." |
| "Savings too good to be true" | "Compare our pilot results against your last inspection. Numbers speak." |
| "What about CAAT regulations?" | "Fully compliant. We handle all permissions — zero burden on you." |
| "Need formal tender" | "Free pilot runs outside procurement. Results feed your evaluation." |
| "Facade damage risk?" | "No contact. Full liability insurance. Zero damage incidents globally." |
| "Want to wait" | "Waiting costs 80-150 THB/sqm for 104K sqm. Pilot eliminates risk." |
| "Multi-level approval needed" | "We have tailored materials for each stakeholder group." |

### Thai Business Culture

- Relationship first — invest in personal connection
- Consensus-driven — consistent messaging across all stakeholders
- Face and hierarchy — frame as "next evolution," never imply current approach is wrong
- Thai-language executive summary
- Patience with persistence — follow up politely and consistently
- PCS as intermediary if stuck
